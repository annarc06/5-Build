A Little Red Sock

In this coming-of-age short film, we follow young lady 'Sally' through her first year at highschool navigating the dramas of the popular circle, the cliches of a small town highschool, and the road bumps that come with growing up.

What Prowls

For the lovers of all things supernatural and out-of-this-world, investigative minds bring you tales from France and their encounter with the grizzly beasts known as werewolves.

I scream For Icecream

Get ready for the thriller of a life-time. In a dark, twisty plot 'I Scream For Icecream' brings you the gruesome story of mysterious disappearances, frightened neighbors, and someone who knows too much, or rather... too little.
